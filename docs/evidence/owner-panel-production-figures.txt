Two of these are worth reading rather than counting.

**Cash received is £29.00 and that is correct, not revenue.** It is the sandbox checkout the
owner completed on 21 September: one order, status active, 2900 minor units. The overview
labels it "measured Payments that have actually settled. Not what has been invoiced", and the
money table carries NET_RECEIPTS_CAVEAT beside the net line. It is not evidence of income and
the page does not say it is.

**Runs, all time, is 0, and so is the 24-hour figure.** Both because every run this deployment
has ever decided belongs to staging; production has taken no verification traffic. A page that
printed a rate here would be inventing one. The workspace count is 1 and that workspace is
`is_synthetic = 1`, so the customers screen shows it with the synthetic badge and the launch
figures exclude it.

=== what this does and does not establish ===

It establishes that the figures behind the panel are real, correctly scoped, and that the two
that could mislead (£29.00 that is not revenue, and zero runs that is not a failure) are
labelled as what they are.

It does not establish that the rendered page in the owner's browser looks right, because that
needs their session. The owner reported on 22 September that they signed in and reached the
panel; nothing they described was broken. The rendering path itself is held by OWNER-927..930
against the same D1 port production uses.
