=== B10. support@itisyou.app ===

DNS is verified and the routing rule is not, because it cannot be read from here.

  MX      route1/2/3.mx.cloudflare.net    (Cloudflare Email Routing)
  SPF     v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net ~all

Whether a rule ACCEPTS support@ is observable from outside, by offering the recipient over
SMTP without sending a message. That was attempted, and the exact refusal is the finding:

  connected to route1.mx.cloudflare.net:25
  <= 550 Sender IP reverse lookup rejected (2a01:4b00:...)

Cloudflare refuses the session before any recipient can be offered, because this machine is
on a connection with no reverse DNS. That is a fact about where the probe runs, not about the
routing rule, and no amount of retrying changes it. A host with reverse DNS could answer the
question; this one cannot.

THE ACTION, in full:
  1. Cloudflare dashboard, itisyou.app, Email, Email Routing, Routes
  2. Confirm a rule whose custom address is support@itisyou.app and whose destination is a
     mailbox you actually read
  3. Send one message to support@itisyou.app from any account and confirm it arrives

Replying AS support@itisyou.app is a separate thing and needs an outbound identity: either
that address verified as a sending identity in Resend, or a "send as" identity in the mail
client. Cloudflare Email Routing is receive-only and cannot send.
