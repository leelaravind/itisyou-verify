ITISYOU Verify

Know whether your automations finished the job.

Your automation says it succeeded. This service goes and looks — reading the CRM record and the email outcome back from the connected systems themselves, then checking them against your rules.

This service is in development and is not yet open for sign-ups.

There is nothing to buy here yet, and no account to create. This page exists so the address resolves while the product is being built in the open.

What it will report

  • Verified — every mandatory check has supporting evidence.
  • Failed — evidence contradicts a rule, or a deadline was missed while evidence access was working.
  • Unverified — access, correlation or evidence is missing or ambiguous. Not a pass, and not a failure.
  • Pending — still inside the agreed completion window.

Absence of evidence never becomes a pass. A workflow's own “success” webhook is a trigger, not proof — that distinction is the whole point.
